## Checked and not a defect

- **"Photos 100" on many albums.** 67 albums hold exactly 100 processed photos in `photo_metadata` (production, read-only count). The number is real, not a display cap.
- **The "127.0.0.1" entry under the site report's visit sources.** It is one real entry visit referred from a local preview, and production records it. It is the gallery owner's own local traffic. Whether to exclude local-preview referrers is a collection rule, not a screen defect.

- A launch in its first days, and an empty gallery: production has none of these right now.

- `/data` on production.
