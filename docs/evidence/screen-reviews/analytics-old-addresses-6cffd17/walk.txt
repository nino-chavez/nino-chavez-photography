No public gallery page was opened (the "public gallery pages" count in each case below is zero).
Labels: "HARNESS OWNER" = not a real sign-in; the preload answers the auth check and the cookie is a fabricated session. "INVENTED" = rows the preload presented as stored for saved views, sharing notes and correction history (production holds none of them: see counts.txt); they carry "(invented)" in their text. Everything else is real production data. Album photos load in this walk (the images come from the real image host); no form was submitted, so nothing was written and no real save, update, note or correction was exercised.
Gates, per capture: overflow compares every element box with the viewport, AND requires that the document itself does not scroll sideways (a box inside a focusable scroller is excused only if the scroller fits and the box is clipped by it: an absolutely positioned box whose scroller is not its containing block escapes the clip and is not excused. The gate used in steps 5 to 7 excused such a box and missed a 559px sideways scroll on the first version of the corrections table; that was found here and fixed). Targets: links, buttons, fields and summaries under 44px; a checkbox is measured as its label; inline links inside sentences are excused and counted. Text 200%: root font size doubled, overflow gate re-run. Keyboard: Tab only (no Enter, Space or clicks); a stop passes if the control or one of its next five ancestors shows a ring. A date field takes four stops (month, day, year, then the browser's own picker button, which draws its own ring: date-picker-focus.png) and is not counted as ring-less. Requests: every non-GET is listed. Console errors exclude image fetches.
Every gate was forced to fail once on purpose on the first page (photos-visitor), at both widths, and fired: a planted box wider than the viewport; a planted 20px button (44px targets); a planted box that fits at 100% text and overflows at 200% (46rem on desktop, 14rem on a phone); a box that escapes its scroller; a button with its outline removed; a planted POST; a planted console error. See the FORCED FAILURE lines under photos-visitor.
Look-and-fix pass: the captures were opened. Fixed because of them: filter labels that picked up a global .field style (dark boxes), the photo dialog's text white on white, photo cards in one column on a phone (7,400px tall, now 2,850px), a "New album" note repeated on every card (now said once), and the corrections history as a table whose actions sat off-screen on a phone (now a list).
